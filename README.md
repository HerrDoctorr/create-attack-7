## ⚡ What Makes Create Attack 7 Special?

### 🏭 Build. Automate. Dominate.
*"Create" is just the baseline. We push it to the absolute limit.*
* **Heavy Machinery:** Go beyond simple cogwheels with **The Factory Must Grow (TFMG)** and **Diesel Generators**. Drill for oil, refine fuel, and forge high-grade steel.
* **Massive Logistics:** Build spanning cross-continental rail networks via **Steam 'n' Rails** and defend them with heavy weaponry from **Create Big Cannons**.
* **Infinite Expansion:** With addons like *Connected*, *Parallel Pipes*, and *Pattern Schematics*, your industrial plant will never run out of space or optimization potential.

### 🦁 A Living World Worth Exploring
*No more tedious, repetitive landscapes.*
* **Next-Gen Biomes:** **Terralith** completely reshapes the Overworld into breathtaking, cinematic vistas, while **Incendium** transforms the Nether into a challenging, fiery underworld.
* **Epic Dungeons:** Every single mine shaft, stronghold, fortress, and monument has been masterfully redesigned using the full **YUNG suite** and *Moog's End Structures*.
* **Vibrant Fauna:** The wilderness is packed with life. Encounter dozens of beautifully animated, dangerous, and majestic creatures from **Alex's Mobs**.

### 🍷 Slow Down and Enjoy Life
*It doesn't always have to be about loud engines and heavy smog.*
* **The "Let's Do" Collection:** Take a break from the factory. Brew fine wine with **Vinery**, bake fresh bread in the **Bakery**, or throw a relaxing summer party with **Beachparty**. 
* **Homely Aesthetics:** Decorate your industrial empire with *Another Furniture*, *Supplementaries*, and *Chipped*. 
* **Capture Memories:** Pack your analog camera from **Exposure**, develop real in-game photos, and hang them up in your base to document your journey.

### 🗂️ Zero Clutter, Pure Efficiency
*A massive factory produces millions of items. We make sure you can handle them.*
* **Digital Inventory:** Power up **Refined Storage** to digitize your items and connect your automated factories directly to a central terminal network.
* **On-The-Go Comfort:** Organize your mass resources with *Storage Drawers* and stay packed for long exploration trips with modular *Sophisticated Backpacks*.

### 🚀 High-End Performance Optimization
*Create Attack 7 is a heavyweight pack—but it runs like butter.*
* Powered by **Embeddium, Oculus, ImmediatelyFast, and FerriteCore**, you get beautiful shader support and maximum FPS, specifically optimized to handle the heavy rendering of massive Create contraptions without breaking a sweat.

## 💻 System Requirements

Running a heavyweight industrial pack with massive automation and complex world generation requires the right setup. To ensure smooth frames and prevent memory issues, please follow these guidelines:

### 🖥️ Client (Player) Settings

| Component | Minimum Requirements | Recommended (For Shaders / Large Factories) |
|---|---|---|
| **Allocated RAM** | **6 GB** *(Absolute minimum to load the pack)* | **8 – 10 GB** *(Sweet spot for smooth gameplay)* |
| **System RAM** | 8 GB | 16 GB or higher |
| **Processor** | Intel Core i5 (8th Gen) / AMD Ryzen 5 2600 | Intel Core i7 (10th Gen) / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X or better |
| **Graphics Card** | NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD RX 580 | NVIDIA RTX 2060 / AMD RX 6600 or better *(Required for Shaders)* |
| **Storage** | 2 GB free space (SSD strongly recommended) | 2 GB free space on an NVMe SSD |

> **⚠️ Important Java Hint:** Make sure you are using **Java 17** (64-bit) and explicitly allocate the RAM in your launcher (e.g., Modrinth App, Prism, or CurseForge). Allocating more than 12 GB is usually not recommended and can cause Java garbage collection stutters.

---

### 🗄️ Server Requirements (If hosting for friends)

Planning to build a sprawling industrial empire together? Here is what your server needs:

* **Small Group (2-4 Players):** 6 – 8 GB dedicated RAM, fast single-core CPU.
* **Large Group (5+ Players + Massive Automation):** 10 – 12 GB dedicated RAM, high-end hoster.
* **Pre-Gen is Key:** We highly recommend using the pre-installed **Chunky** mod to pre-generate your world chunks before letting players explore. *Terralith* and *Incendium* generate beautiful but heavy terrain!
